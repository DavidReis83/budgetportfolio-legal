Personal Use

This setup is intended for the operator's personal, non-commercial use. You must use only accounts and data that you are authorized to access.

Read-Only Financial Data

The connection is intended to retrieve account information and transactions. It does not authorize payments or change your bank account. Always review imported transactions and balances for accuracy.

Availability and Accuracy

The setup may be unavailable or may receive incomplete, delayed, or incorrectly formatted data from a bank or open-banking provider. The operator is responsible for checking imported information against the bank's records.
