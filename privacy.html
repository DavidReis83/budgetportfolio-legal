Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-11

BudgetPortfolio is a private, personal budgeting setup used to import read-only financial data into a self-hosted Actual Budget instance.

Data We Process

With your permission, Enable Banking may provide account information, balances, account identifiers, and transaction data from your selected bank account. The data is used only for personal budgeting, transaction review, reconciliation, and reporting.

How Data Is Used

Data is imported into the user's private Actual Budget installation. It is not sold, rented, used for advertising, or used to make lending or other automated financial decisions.

Service Providers

Enable Banking provides the open-banking connection. Your bank performs the authentication and consent process. Actual Budget provides the budgeting software. Each service may have its own privacy policy and terms. This page does not replace those policies.

Storage and Retention

The self-hosted Actual Budget instance is controlled by the user. The user is responsible for server access, backups, retention, and deletion of imported data. Bank access can be revoked through the bank or Enable Banking connection flow.

Security

Private keys, passwords, tokens, and banking credentials must not be published on this site or stored in public source repositories.

Your Choices

You may stop the connection, revoke consent, remove imported data, or contact the operator about privacy questions.
